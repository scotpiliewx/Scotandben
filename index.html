Who is Scot Pilie?

Born and raised with hot sauce in his veins, Scot Pilie has been eating his way through New Orleans one video at a time. From hole-in-the-wall po'boy joints to James Beard-nominated dining rooms — if it's worth your time, Scot's already been there.

What started as a love letter to his city has grown into a trusted guide for locals and visitors alike, and now an expanding tour of the South's most delicious destinations.
